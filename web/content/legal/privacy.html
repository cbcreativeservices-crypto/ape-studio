1. Introduction and Privacy Commitment

This Privacy Policy explains how Pro Audio Training Academy (“Pro Audio Training Academy,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, protects, and retains information when an individual accesses or uses our mobile applications, websites, browser-based learning environment, customer-support channels, and related educational services (collectively, the “Services”).

This Policy applies to the free reference experience and the paid Academy experience. It also applies when a person creates an account, communicates with us, requests support, participates in testing or research, purchases access through an application marketplace, receives a certificate, or elects to use an optional networking or credential-verification feature.

Our baseline privacy commitments are:

  • We do not sell Personal Information.
  • We do not operate the Services as an advertising platform.
  • We do not use third-party advertising networks within the learning experience.
  • We seek to collect only information reasonably necessary to operate, secure, support, and improve the Services.
  • We seek to keep this Policy, application-store disclosures, permission prompts, and actual production behavior consistent.

The Services are intended for users who are at least 13 years old and are not directed to children under 13. Additional requirements may apply when a minor uses the Services through a school, institution, parent, or guardian.

By using the Services, a user acknowledges that information will be handled as described in this Policy. Where consent is legally required for a specific practice, we will request it separately. This Policy does not replace a more specific notice presented at the point of collection, in a device permission prompt, or in connection with an optional feature.

2. Definitions

Account means a registered user profile used to access paid or personalized features, save progress, synchronize information, or obtain certificates or other records.

Academy Mode means the paid or otherwise authorized portion of the Services that provides access to the complete curriculum, guided study features, assessments, progress tracking, and other premium functions.

Certificate means a completion record issued after a user satisfies the applicable learning, study, assessment, and integrity requirements.

Credential means a Certificate, achievement, verification record, or other representation that a user has satisfied specified Academy requirements.

Deidentified Information means information that cannot reasonably be used to infer information about, or otherwise be linked to, a particular person when maintained with appropriate safeguards.

Device Data means technical information about a device, application, or browser, such as device type, operating system, application version, browser type, language, and diagnostic identifiers.

Institution means a school, college, university, training provider, employer, or other organization that obtains or administers access to the Services.

Networking Profile means an optional profile intended for professional networking or employer-facing functions and made available only when the user affirmatively participates.

Personal Information means information that identifies, relates to, describes, is reasonably capable of being associated with, or could reasonably be linked to a particular person or household, subject to applicable law.

Purchase Entitlement means a record showing whether a user has a monthly, annual, lifetime, promotional, institutional, or other right to access Academy Mode. It does not include a complete payment-card number.

Reference Mode means the free portion of the Services that may be used without creating an Account and that includes the professional-audio glossary and other free content or tools made available by us.

Service Provider means a company or individual that processes information on our behalf to provide hosting, authentication, analytics, diagnostics, billing support, communications, security, or other operational services.

Usage Data means information about how the Services are accessed and used, such as screens viewed, features selected, sessions, navigation paths, study activity, and technical performance events.

User means any person who accesses or uses any portion of the Services, whether as a guest, registered user, subscriber, certificate holder, tester, or institutional participant.

4. Information We Collect

A user may provide:

Users should not submit sensitive information that is unnecessary for the requested service. Users should not send government identification numbers, financial account credentials, medical records, or similarly sensitive material through ordinary support email unless we specifically request an appropriate verification item and provide a suitable submission method.

For registered users, we may collect and maintain:

For purchases processed through the Apple App Store or Google Play, we may receive:

  • Internet Protocol address and general network information. An Internet Protocol address may indicate an approximate region, but we do not use it to collect precise Global Positioning System location.

4.5 Usage and Analytics Information

Usage Data may include:

  • Screens or pages opened and the order in which they are viewed.
  • Buttons, navigation items, searches, glossary entries, audio tools, and learning activities used.
  • Session frequency, duration, return activity, and aggregate retention measures.

  • Load time, failed requests, synchronization results, and application responsiveness.
  • General campaign or referral information associated with a website or application-store link.

Analytics should be configured not to collect passwords, complete support messages, raw microphone recordings, or unnecessary content from free-text fields.

4.6 Microphone and Audio-Tool Information

The application may request microphone access only when a user selects an audio measurement or educational tool that requires live sound input. Depending on the final implementation, the tool may process audio on the device to display sound level, peak level, root mean square level, spectrum, waveform, spectrogram, reverberation estimates, or related measurements.

Unless an updated notice expressly states otherwise, we do not intend to record, store, or transmit continuous raw microphone audio to our servers. Application-store disclosures and this Policy must be revised before a future feature uploads, stores, shares, or analyzes raw audio off the device.

4.7 Optional Profile Photograph and Networking Information

A profile photograph and professional-profile information are optional.
