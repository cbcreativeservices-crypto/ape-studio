## Technical corrections (Agent 1, High confidence)

1. **amplifier classes** — Class B efficiency corrected to the accepted theoretical max (**~78.5%**, was "75%"); Class A clarified (~25% capacitive / up to 50% transformer-coupled). Definition de-bloated.
2. **Asymmetrical clipping** — was "produces even-order harmonics"; corrected to **"adds even-order harmonics on top of the odd-order harmonics clipping already produces"** (the distinguishing trait vs symmetrical clipping).
3. **all-pass filter** — over-long, circular definition tightened to one precise statement (passes all frequencies at equal amplitude, shifts phase → frequency-dependent delay without tonal change).
