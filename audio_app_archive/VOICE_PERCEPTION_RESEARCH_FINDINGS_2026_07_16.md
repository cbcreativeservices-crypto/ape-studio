## CORRECTIONS to current 59 (both agents concur on 1–2)
1. **Vocal Formant** — redundant / non-standard (F1, F2, Higher Formants, Singer's Formant already cover it). → **Rename to "Speaker's Formant"** (a distinct ~3–3.5 kHz spoken-voice ring for actors/broadcasters) OR drop.
2. **Tremolo** — mislabeled as a vibrato synonym. In voice science it's a **vibrato fault** (too fast & narrow — the "bleat"). → Redefine as such; note healthy vibrato ≈ 4.5–6.5 Hz, ~50–120 cents; distinct from instrumental amplitude tremolo.
3. **Higher Formants** → specify **F3–F5**.
4. **Singer's Formant** → define precisely: **F3–F5 cluster ~2.8–3.4 kHz** from **epilaryngeal narrowing**.
5. **Hoarseness / Roughness / Breathiness / Strained Voice** → anchor to the standard **GRBAS** perceptual scale (keeps them perceptual, not clinical).
6. **Chest Voice / Head Voice** → annotate as the lower/upper portions of the **modal register** (TA- vs CT-dominant), not separate registers.
7. **Falsetto** → note it ≠ head voice (distinct lighter register); add **Reinforced Falsetto**.
8. **Mixed Voice** → note it's a **blended registration** (pedagogically contested as a discrete register).
