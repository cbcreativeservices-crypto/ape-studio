## Technical corrections (Agent 1)

1. **Aux-fed subwoofer (Major)** — definition described a home-theater AVR/bass-management setup; in the course's live-sound context it means feeding subs from a dedicated **post-fader aux send** carrying only selected LF sources (kick/bass/toms) to cut mud and intermodulation. Rewrote Concise + Plain-English. *High confidence (Fulcrum Acoustic, FOH Magazine, Yamaha Pro Audio).*
2. **automatic mic mixer** — circular definition; now covers both methods (**gain-sharing/Dugan vs gating**) and NOM (number of open mics).
3. **Boundary microphone** — "integrated electronics compensating for boundary reflections" overstated; corrected to a **pressure-zone/placement** effect that avoids comb filtering.
4. **band-limiting filters / boost-cut EQ** — vague/circular definitions tightened with precise corner-frequency and gain/Q language.
