## Technical corrections — 3 factual Majors (Agent 1, High confidence)

1. **C-weighting** — definition falsely claimed it "emphasizes mid-range (1–4 kHz) and de-emphasizes lows/highs." C-weighting is **nearly flat (~31.5 Hz–8 kHz)** with gentle roll-off only at the extremes. The error **contradicted the term's own Common Mistakes field**. Verified vs IEC 61672. Concise, Plain-English, Purpose, and Common Mistakes rewritten. *(This is the error the committee flagged during the earlier Batch 2 pass — now fixed in its home batch.)*
2. **Capsule polarization voltage** — claimed "typically 48 volts," conflating the 48 V **phantom supply** with the actual capsule **polarizing voltage**, which is stepped up internally to **~60–80 V**. Rewrote Plain-English + Common Mistakes; added the electret contrast.
3. **Chorus Effect** — Practical Application gave "100–300 ms delay, 5–15% feedback." Chorus base delay is **~15–35 ms** (100–300 ms is slapback/echo) and **feedback is a flanger parameter**, not standard chorus. Corrected ranges (15–35 ms, 0.1–2 Hz rate); removed the spurious feedback reference.

Plus 3 Minor technical tightenings: **Butterworth** (Purpose overstated "minimizes phase shift," contradicting its own body text), **buffer amplifier**, **Bus**.
