## Most important technical corrections (High confidence)

- **Ribbon transformer (Major).** Claimed the ribbon is "150–300 Ω" stepping up to "line-level (10k+ Ω)." Both wrong: the bare ribbon is **sub-ohm (~0.1–0.2 Ω)**; 150–300 Ω is the *post-transformer* output, which is balanced **mic level (~200–600 Ω), not line level**. Corrected (turns ratio ~1:20–1:40, impedance scales as ratio²).
- **Microphone sensitivity** — given as "+44 dBV/Pa"; dBV mic sensitivity is referenced to 1 V/Pa and is **always negative** (~**−44 dBV/Pa** ≈ 6.3 mV/Pa). A positive value would imply ~158 V from a mic. Corrected.
- **Single-diaphragm capsule** — wrongly stated "dual-diaphragm designs like some ribbon mics use two elements." Ribbon mics use a **single** corrugated ribbon; dual-diaphragm is a **condenser** construction. Corrected.
- **Ribbon motor** — "electromagnetic field coils" removed; a ribbon motor uses **permanent magnets only**.
- **Smaart LE** — described as "free"; it is a **paid**, feature-reduced edition. Corrected.
- Plus precision fixes to Reverberation (duplicated clause), Smaart Suite/RT (overstated prediction/marketing claims), loudspeaker sensitivity (+10 dB ≈ twice as loud, not +3 dB), and SPL (pressure vs intensity).
