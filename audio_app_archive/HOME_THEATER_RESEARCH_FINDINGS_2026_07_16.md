3. **"Bass Trap"** — distinguish **porous** (broadband) vs **membrane/Helmholtz** (tuned). *(Agent A)*
4. **"THX Certification"** — specify the **tiers** (Compact/Select/Ultra/Dominus, by room volume). *(both)*
5. **"HDCP"** → "HDCP (2.2/2.3 for 4K/HDR)". *(Agent B)*
