## CORRECTIONS — factual precision (apply to existing terms; occupational expert)
1. **PEL vs Action Level** (commonly swapped): OSHA **PEL = 90 dBA TWA** (5 dB exchange, 115 dBA ceiling, 100% dose); **Action Level = 85 dBA TWA** (triggers the Hearing Conservation Program — *not* the limit). NIOSH **REL = 85 dBA / 3 dB**.
2. **Criterion Level** = the TWA equal to 100% dose (90 dBA OSHA / 85 dBA NIOSH) — state which.
3. **Exchange Rate authority**: OSHA uses **5 dB**; NIOSH + EU/ISO use **3 dB** (equal-energy, physically correct). Don't present 5 dB as best practice.
4. **Peak Sound Pressure Level** = **C-weighted (LCpeak)**; OSHA impulse ceiling 140 dB peak; ≠ Lmax (time-weighted).
5. **NRR** overstates field protection → apply **(NRR−7)/2** (A-weighted) or NIOSH derating (×0.75 muffs / ×0.50 foam / ×0.30 other plugs).
6. **SNR ≠ NRR** — SNR (EU/ISO, C−A) reads a few dB higher; convert, don't equate.
7. **Double Hearing Protection** does **not** sum NRRs — NIOSH ≈ higher device + 5 dB.
8. **Helmet-Mounted Hearing Protection** → standard term is **Cap-/Hard-Hat-Mounted Earmuff**.
9. (minor) **Stage Noise Management** — add alias **"Stage Volume"** for searchability.
