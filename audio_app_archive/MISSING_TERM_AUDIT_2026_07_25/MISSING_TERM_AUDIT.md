## Notes on my curation choices
- **Parent over variants.** Your list had ~15 "___ Liner" and several "___ Donut" / "___ Rotation" / "___ Category" forms. I recommend the *parent* term (Liner, Donut, Clockwheel) and skip the micro-variants, which a definition can enumerate.
- **Rotation tiers kept.** *Current / Recurrent / Gold / Power* rotation are standard music-director vocabulary, so they stay as distinct entries.
- **Pseudoscience excluded.** Directional cables, cable elevators, cryo-treatment, dielectric bias, etc. — excluded as controversial for a certification product (can be covered *critically* if you want a "myths" area).
- **Brand features mostly skipped.** Avid/SSL/UA/RME feature names are vendor-specific. I kept only 3 near-generic ones as *optional* (All-Buttons-In / 1176, Unison, Listen Mic).
- **Consolidations to decide at authoring:** *Baerwald / Löfgren / Stevenson* → one "Cartridge Alignment Geometry" entry; *Record Clamp / Weight* → one; *RDS / RBDS* → one; *Morning/Afternoon/Evening Drive* → covered under *Drive Time* + *Morning Drive*.
