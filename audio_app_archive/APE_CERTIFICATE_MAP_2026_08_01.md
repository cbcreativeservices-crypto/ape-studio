_2026-08-01 · Machine A · derived from live DB + planned Computer C terms_

## Model
- **Specialization Certificate (L1)** = one of the **23 clustered specializations** below.
- **Diploma (L2)** = **Music** or **Audio** (a diploma bundles the specializations on its track). Audio is intentionally the broader track.
- **Masters (L3)** = top tier above the diplomas.
- **Requisites (reminder markers, not gates):** Professional Audio Safety + Workplace Skills (+ Grounding & Electrical for the paid tracks); Foundations of Sound gateway lab.
- Counts: **Now** = live in DB (17,391 total); **Incoming** = net-new Computer C terms still to author (1,933); **Projected** = 19,324.
