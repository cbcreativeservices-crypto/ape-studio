Topic Foundations: Active Devices and Semiconductors

A microphone makes a very weak electrical signal, but a loudspeaker needs a much larger one. Making a signal larger needs a part that takes energy from a power supply. A part that uses power to control a signal is called an active component. So how can a weak signal control a much larger current?

The most important active component is the transistor. It is usually made from specially treated silicon. It usually has three wire connections. A small signal at one of them controls a much bigger current through the other two. In one main type, a small current at the control connection does this. In the other type, a voltage does it, and almost no current flows into that connection. A transistor can make a signal larger, or work as a switch that is fully on or off.

Designers connect a few transistors into standard groups called stages. Each stage does one job. An input stage often uses two matched transistors that share one current. They compare two inputs, amplify the difference, and largely reject noise that is the same on both. That group is called a differential pair.

An output stage has a different job, which is to deliver a large current to the loudspeaker. In a power amplifier, two transistors usually share this work, one for each half of the sound wave. They become very hot. The range of voltage and current a transistor can handle together without failing is called the safe operating area.

Many stages can be built together on one small piece of silicon, called an integrated circuit, or chip. The most common amplifier chip is the operational amplifier. It amplifies the difference between its two inputs by a very large amount. A few parts around it set how much larger the output signal becomes. That amount is the gain.

Not every chip makes a signal larger. Some chips compare two voltages and show which is larger. Converter chips change a sound signal into numbers that a computer can store, and change them back again.

Those numbers are handled by digital circuits. They use transistors as switches with only two states, which stand for one and zero. A small group of these switches can give an output that depends on its inputs by a fixed rule. Another small group can hold one bit of memory, a single one or zero. Some memory keeps its data when the power is off. A tiny complete computer on one chip is called a microcontroller. It runs a small program, for example one that reads the buttons and shows the menus.

The flashcards explain each of these parts in more detail, from single transistors to complete chips. By the end, you will know how active parts let a small signal control a larger current, and how they are combined into the chips inside audio equipment.
