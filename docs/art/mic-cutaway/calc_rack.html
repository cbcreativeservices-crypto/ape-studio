▸ WHAT ARE YOU CALCULATING?
SPL at a distance
L₂ = L₁ − 20·log₁₀(d₂/d₁)
Distance for a target SPL
d₂ = d₁·10^((L₁−L₂)/20)
▾ WHY THIS MATTERS
Every doubling of distance drops a point-source level by 6 dB. Front-row to back-row math starts here — and it's why one box rarely covers a room.
▸ COMMON MISTAKES
• Forgetting the 6 dB / doubling rule only holds in the free field.
