READOUT

Generator, not motor: the sound pressure p drives the diaphragm; the coil moves in the gap and e = Bl·v is induced — proportional to VELOCITY, not displacement. A well-damped dynamic capsule is resistance-controlled, so v follows p and the output waveform follows the pressure waveform; displacement lags by 90°. Shown amplitude is exaggerated ~10³× (real: sub-µm to µm). Field breathes on its own 4 s clock: it is permanent. Motor radial proportions ≈ true; gap/coil stretched for legibility; wavelength not to scale.
