# Lab Images — Reference-Search Queries (Tier 1 & 2) — 2026-08-16

Companion to `APE_LAB_IMAGES_TIER1-2_2026_08_16.md`. Item numbers match 1:1.

**Purpose:** gather a real-world visual reference for each object *before*
generating it, so each house-style image is built against how the thing actually
looks — not the model's guess.

## Workflow (per item)

1. **Search** the query below in an image search engine (Google Images or Bing
   Images give the densest grids). Add the negative terms where noted.
2. **Screenshot the results grid** — the full collage of many examples at once,
   so proportions and common features are visible side by side.
3. **Hand it to ChatGPT** with the reference instruction below, then paste the
   matching generation prompt from the main package.
4. **Generate.** Do the reference pass for a whole stage first, then generate the
   stage — keeps the batch consistent.

## Paste-ready reference instruction (use with every screenshot)

> Attached is a grid of real-world reference photos of **[ITEM NAME]**. Study
> the shared form, proportions, materials, and identifying features across these
> examples — that is what the object actually looks like. Then generate our image
> using the prompt I paste next, in our house style: single generic unbranded
> unit, seamless pure-white background, three-quarter product-photo angle. Match
> the real object's form and detail to these references, but do not copy any
> single photo and do not reproduce any brand marks, logos, or model text.

**"Look for" note:** each item lists the features to confirm are present in your
chosen reference — the same "must show" points the generation prompt enforces.
If the grid is mostly the wrong variant, use the alternate query.

# STAGE 1 — TIER 1 (12)

### 1. Moving-coil dynamic microphone — `dynamic-microphone.webp`
- **Search:** `moving coil dynamic microphone`
- **Alt:** `dynamic vocal microphone stage`
- **Look for:** rounded metal mesh grille, solid tapered metal body, no visible large diaphragm. Skip results that are clearly large-diaphragm condensers.

### 2. Ribbon microphone — `ribbon-microphone.webp`
- **Search:** `ribbon microphone studio`
- **Look for:** side-address body with a wide flat front grille; premium/vintage look. Both classic and modern cylindrical bodies are valid references.

### 3. Small-diaphragm "pencil" condenser — `small-diaphragm-condenser.webp`
- **Search:** `small diaphragm condenser microphone pencil`
- **Look for:** slim, straight, even-diameter cylinder with a small end-address capsule. Contrast against the fat large-diaphragm studio mic (don't pick those).

### 4. Electret condenser capsule — `electret-capsule.webp`
- **Search:** `electret condenser microphone capsule`
- **Alt:** `electret microphone capsule component`
- **Look for:** tiny brass/metal cylinder, perforated front sound port, solder terminals or wire leads at the back. It is a bare component — reject finished microphones.

### 5. Line array (flown) — `line-array.webp`
- **Search:** `line array loudspeaker concert flown`
- **Alt:** `line array PA hanging rig`
- **Look for:** vertical column of identical trapezoidal cabinets, splayed into a J-curve, hung from a fly frame.

### 6. Subwoofer cabinet — `subwoofer.webp`
- **Search:** `live sound subwoofer cabinet PA` **`-car -home`**
- **Look for:** large squat rectangular box, protective grille, one big driver or a large port. Exclude car-audio and home-theater subs.

### 7. Passive crossover network — `crossover.webp`
- **Search:** `passive speaker crossover network board` **`-car -suv`**  *(negatives are essential — "crossover" alone returns vehicles)*
- **Alt:** `loudspeaker crossover components inductor capacitor`
- **Look for:** a circuit board with large inductor coils, capacitors, and wire terminals. (If you'd rather show an active rack crossover instead, tell me and I'll respec.)

### 8. Acoustic foam panel (wedge) — `acoustic-foam.webp`
- **Search:** `acoustic foam panel studio wedge`
- **Look for:** square foam tile with regular wedge/pyramid peaks and valleys.

### 9. Broadband absorber panel — `fiberglass-panel.webp`
- **Search:** `broadband acoustic absorber panel fabric`
- **Alt:** `fabric wrapped acoustic panel rockwool`
- **Look for:** thick rectangular fabric-wrapped rigid panel; bonus if a reference shows the mineral-wool/fiberglass core.

### 10. Bass trap — `bass-trap.webp`
- **Search:** `corner bass trap acoustic`
- **Look for:** tall triangular or columnar unit shaped to fit a room corner.

### 11. Electric bass guitar — `bass-guitar.webp`
- **Search:** `4 string electric bass guitar`
- **Alt:** `electric bass guitar full body`
- **Look for:** four strings, long fretted neck with markers, pickups and bridge on the body, tuners on the headstock.

### 12. Modular / eurorack synthesizer — `modular-synth.webp`
- **Search:** `eurorack modular synthesizer`
- **Alt:** `modular synth patch cables rack`
- **Look for:** a case holding several distinct modules; dense knobs and jack sockets; patch cables looping between modules.

# STAGE 2 — TIER 2 (11)

### 13. Mixing console / channel strip — `mixing-console.webp`
- **Search:** `analog mixing console studio`
- **Alt:** `audio mixing desk channel strips`
- **Look for:** multiple channels, each with a vertical fader and a column of knobs; a master section. Prefer a compact desk over a giant one.

### 14. Microphone preamp — `mic-preamp.webp`
- **Search:** `microphone preamp rack unit`
- **Alt:** `outboard mic preamp hardware`
- **Look for:** prominent gain knob, a phantom-power switch, a level meter (VU or LED), XLR input.

### 15. Compressor (rack) — `compressor.webp`
- **Search:** `rack mount audio compressor hardware`
- **Look for:** threshold / ratio / attack / release / makeup knobs and a gain-reduction meter (needle or LED).

### 16. Parametric equalizer — `parametric-eq.webp`
- **Search:** `parametric equalizer rack hardware`
- **Alt:** `outboard parametric EQ unit`
- **Look for:** controls grouped into bands as clusters of rotary knobs — NOT a bank of vertical sliders (that's a graphic EQ).

### 17. Digital-to-analog converter — `dac.webp`
- **Search:** `standalone audio DAC converter unit`
- **Alt:** `digital to analog converter studio rack`
- **Look for:** digital inputs (optical/coax/AES), analog output jacks, a small display or clock/lock LEDs.

### 18. Noise gate — `noise-gate.webp`
- **Search:** `noise gate rack unit audio`
- **Alt:** `hardware gate expander outboard`
- **Look for:** threshold / range / attack / hold / release knobs and open/closed status LEDs.

### 19. Limiter (brickwall) — `limiter.webp`
- **Search:** `brickwall limiter rack hardware`
- **Alt:** `hardware audio limiter outboard`
- **Look for:** threshold / output-ceiling controls and a bold gain-reduction meter. Should read as its own unit, not a twin of the compressor.

### 20. Reverb unit — `reverb-unit.webp`
- **Search:** `rack reverb unit hardware`
- **Alt:** `digital reverb processor rack`
- **Look for:** large data/value wheel, small display, program-select buttons.

### 21. Delay unit — `delay-unit.webp`
- **Search:** `rack delay unit hardware`
- **Alt (pedal option):** `delay guitar pedal`
- **Look for:** value wheel, display, feedback/mix knobs, tap-tempo button. (Default is the rack unit; the pedal alt is if you prefer that idiom.)

### 22. Modulation effects pedal — `modulation-fx.webp`
- **Search:** `chorus flanger phaser guitar pedal`
- **Alt:** `modulation guitar effects pedal`
- **Look for:** compact stompbox, rate/depth knobs, a large footswitch, status LED.

### 23. Distortion / saturation pedal — `distortion-unit.webp`
- **Search:** `distortion overdrive guitar pedal`
- **Look for:** compact stompbox, drive/tone/level knobs, a large footswitch, status LED.
