# Cable Lab Artwork Prompts — Digital / Network / Control Family (2026-08-16)

Companion to `docs/art/APE_CABLE_ART_SPEC_2026_08_16.md` — the master spec governs
style, naming, canvas sizes, and delivery. Every physical claim below is pulled from
the B2-verified records in `src/screens/lab/cable/data/connectors.digital.ts`.

**Connectors (10 core):** `usb_a`, `usb_b`, `usb_micro_b`, `usb_c`, `ethernet_8p8c`,
`ethercon_style`, `bnc`, `toslink`, `hdmi`, `midi_din5`.

**Prompt count: 52** — 40 standard views (10 × side / face-plug / face-recept /
mating), 2 extra USB 3.x teaching side views, 10 optional T5 simplified exploded views.

Global reminders (full rules: master spec §2–§3):

- Transparent PNG. SIDE / MATING / exploded: 1600×1000. FACE views: 1000×1000.
- Key light upper-left, soft rim light lower-right (objects sit on near-black UI).
- SIDE views: cable exits LEFT, mating end points RIGHT, horizontal, ~80% fill.
- FACE views: dead-on; latch/keyway at 12 o'clock unless stated. Plug and
  receptacle faces are SEPARATE assets — never mirror one to make the other.
- MATING views: plug on the RIGHT approaching receptacle on the LEFT, gap of about
  half a connector length, three-quarter angle allowed.
- Unbranded, no logos, NO text or numbers baked into the image, no damage/dirt.

**Receptacle staging note (this family):** every receptacle here lives on equipment,
not on a cable — so `face-recept` and `mating` prompts stage the receptacle set into
a small neutral matte-dark device-panel fragment (a simple abstract panel, no full
device, no branding).

## Judgment calls flagged to the owner

1. **Extra views added** (record-driven, filenames extend the master-spec four-name
   scheme): `usb_b__side-usb3.png` (the record teaches 3.x-vs-2.0 B mating
   incompatibility in three separate fields — art strongly needed) and
   `usb_micro_b__side-usb3.png` (taught in notInterchangeableWith; LOWER priority —
   the record calls the variant rare in audio equipment). Confirm the filenames
   before integration.
2. **T5 filenames** use `<connectorId>__exploded.png` — consistent with the scene
   naming style but not explicitly listed in master spec §2; confirm.
3. **HDMI trapezoid orientation** (longer edge at 12 o'clock) is a consistency pick
   for this family, not a verified standard orientation.
4. **BNC female lugs rendered at 3/9 o'clock** — rotationally arbitrary for two
   opposed lugs; chosen for a stable read.
5. **8P8C left-to-right pin direction** in face views is deferred to real
   references + the face-geometry verification pass (mirror trap); the records
   verify pair colors and order but not rendered direction.
6. **USB tongue/contact orientation** (which half, which way contacts face) is not
   in the records — all USB prompts defer to real product references.
7. **Neutral colors on USB variants**: the informal blue-insulator cue for USB 3.x
   is NOT record-verified, so the 3.x prompts rely on shape and forbid color-coding.
8. **TOSLINK receptacle rendered flapless** — many consumer ports have spring
   flaps; the uncovered style was chosen so the lens and keyed opening teach.
9. **Micro-B 3.x side view allows a slight three-quarter angle** (deviation from
   the strict side convention) so the double-width two-section tip can read.
10. **Receptacle staging**: all receptacles in this family are equipment-mounted,
    so face-recept/mating views stage them in a neutral matte-dark panel fragment
    (no full device) — an adaptation the master spec's cable-to-cable XLR
    exemplars did not need.
