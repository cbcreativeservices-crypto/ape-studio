Auditor G5, 2026-11-14. Web preview `http://localhost:8142/#labpreview/StagePlotLab` (opened as `/?g5=1#labpreview/StagePlotLab` to force a fresh load), tab 390 x 844, member, intro cards unseen. Walked as a beginner from a BLANK stage (band "Test Trio", one performer, one guitar amplifier with its microphone, one wedge mix), then opened the FOUR-PIECE BAND example. PRINT, DOWNLOAD PDF, SHARE, EMAIL and the COPY buttons were never pressed. No sound in this lab.

This is a different kind of lab from the Game Audio Lab: a working tool (a form and a drawing) with five tabs (SETUP · STAGE · INPUTS · MONITORS · EXPORT), not a paged lesson. Each tab has an intro card ("WHAT YOU'RE LEARNING / WHERE TO START"), and each has ⓘ explainers inline.

## STAGE PLOT LAB · overall verdict

**The beginner's journey.** The library is calm and the purpose sentence is clear. From a blank stage, the beginner meets a long form in SETUP, then a beautifully labelled stage drawing, then an add flow that teaches "a symbol is not an input" by asking what each symbol stands for, then an input list and a monitor plan that ask questions ("Is that intended?") rather than scold, and an export page that prints open questions instead of hiding them. The tool is honest and thorough; what a beginner lacks is a single guided path: the five-step guided task that would carry them through is hidden and labelled optional, a few hand-offs between tabs do not do what their button says, and every tab shows its full professional depth on day one.

**3 biggest orientation problems**
1. **The guided path is hidden.** GUIDED TASK (OPTIONAL) is closed by default, the "? n" tab markers are unexplained, and the library does not say "start with Blank stage to learn".
2. **Full professional depth shown at once.** SETUP (~25 fields), INPUTS (DI devices, stage boxes, reserved channels, renumber, splits, backline…) and the three zero-counts on MONITORS appear before the learner has one row.
3. **Hand-offs that mislead.** Setup's "PLACE ON THE STAGE" does not place; the Stage card says "ADD → Performer" when a performer already waits to be placed; new objects stack on the same spot; the palette re-opens after an add so the result is not seen; the Export card names a SHARE PDF button the web screen does not have.

**3 best things**
1. **"What does this symbol stand for?"** The add flow (package → what it assumes → what inputs it adds → "Nothing is added until you say so") teaches the lab's core idea by doing.
2. **Plain-English help at the point of need.** The words popup, the "Add a mix" sheet glossary lines, "Ready to advance … does not mean the venue has agreed", "stage right is on your left" drawn on the frame.
3. **Questions, not errors.** "Performer 1 has no monitor. Is that intended?", REVIEW with GO TO ›, "Nothing is filled in for you", open details printed in the packet.

**"First 30 seconds" rewrite**
Library: show the purpose open ("A stage plot tells the venue's sound engineer who stands where, what to plug in, and what each performer needs to hear, before you arrive."), with a small picture of a finished plot. Then one recommended button: "LEARN IT: START FROM A BLANK STAGE (5 guided steps)". In the plot, open the five steps as a pinned checklist at the top of every tab with the current step highlighted, and keep each tab's advanced sections folded until the step that needs them.
