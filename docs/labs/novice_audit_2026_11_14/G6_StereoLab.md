## Lab verdict — StereoLab

**Beginner's journey:** The card is concrete ("point to where each instrument sounds like it is") and the caption gives a two-step recipe whose result — two waves merging into one — is visible without sound. The words around it (M/S, goniometer, decorrelated, three names for mono) slow the beginner down, and the check can be passed by picking the longest answer.

**3 biggest orientation problems**
1. Three names for the mono action; goniometer vs lissajous across labs.
2. MID / SIDE never glossed on the EXPLORE screen.
3. CHECK answers give themselves away by length (4 of 4).

**3 best things**
1. Recipe caption → visible merge.
2. "Wider sounds bigger — until you fold to mono".
3. The state-aware WHAT'S LEFT end screen.

**First 30 seconds rewrite**
Card as now. Pointer on WIDTH: "Drag right — the two sides grow apart (wider)." Then MONO CHECK: "This is how a phone speaker hears your mix. See what's left?" Correlation gauge: green = safe, red = will vanish.
