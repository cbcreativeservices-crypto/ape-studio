## Lab verdict — Sound Playground

**Beginner's journey.** A newcomer who taps this tile straight from the Labs menu lands in a synthesizer with three live views, four readouts and two option trays holding about 25 choices, most named in jargon (SAW, PINK, POLARITY, LPF, Q). The intro card's two concrete steps rescue the first minute, and every change visibly ripples through all views, which is satisfying. After that there is no path, only an open workspace, so the beginner pokes a few buttons, meets a CHECK YOURSELF about low-pass filters they were never shown, and leaves. Someone who finished Foundations of Sound first will find it a good review space.

**3 biggest orientation problems**
1. It does not say it is meant AFTER Foundations of Sound; from the menu it looks like a starting point.
2. Too many controls at once (SOURCE ~17 options, FX ~10), named in jargon, with no "basic first" stage.
3. The page's loudest instruction is "Press PLAY" (needs sound) and its text includes a developer note ("update the app to hear the full sound") and cross-lab "Module 10" references.

**3 best things**
1. TRY THIS with ↻ ANOTHER and "predict first" prompts.
2. Instant, consistent feedback across AIR, WAVEFORM and SPECTRUM for every change.
3. The intro card's concrete first two steps (λ at 290 → 580 Hz; SQUARE → count the spectrum lines).

**First 30 seconds rewrite.** Card: "This is the Foundations of Sound toolbox, all on one screen. If you have not done that lab yet, start there (link). Here, change one thing at a time and watch the three pictures: the air, the wave and the spectrum." First view shows only FREQ, LEVEL and four wave shapes, with TRY THIS directly under the display: "Tap SQUARE. Did the corners get sharper? Count the bars in SPECTRUM." A "More controls" button opens noise, harmonics, phase and effects once the learner has tried two prompts.
