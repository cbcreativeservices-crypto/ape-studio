## Lab verdict — Speaker Placement & Coverage

**Beginner's journey.** The friendliest lab in my set. The first screen shows a room with half the seats dark and a single obvious fix (add a second speaker); the flashlight analogy and the colour key in plain words make the map readable in seconds. Module 2 changes to a side view without saying so, which costs a moment of confusion, and adds five controls. Module 3 is the vocabulary the first two modules already used. A beginner finishes in a few minutes knowing what coverage, hot spots and dead zones are and why speakers are flown and tilted.

**3 biggest orientation problems**
1. The view flips from top-down (Module 1) to side view (Module 2) with no label.
2. The vocabulary page comes last, after the words were already used; the colour key is printed twice.
3. Hidden selection: SPKR 2 changes which speaker the sliders move without a visible "editing" cue on the map; two identical ⓘ icons in the header.

**3 best things**
1. "A loudspeaker works like a flashlight for sound."
2. A coverage map whose dark seats state the problem before any reading.
3. READING THE COLORS in plain words, with real-size people and a 5 m bar.

**First 30 seconds rewrite.** Card: "A speaker is a flashlight for sound. Dark seats can't hear well. Your job: light up every seat." Map opens with one speaker and the dark half visible; a pulsing hint on SPKR 2: "Add a second speaker". The colour key sits in one line under the map ("red = too loud · green = just right · black = can't hear"). Module 2 opens with a "SIDE VIEW" label and only HEIGHT and TILT; ROOM and SYSTEM unlock after the colour evens out.
