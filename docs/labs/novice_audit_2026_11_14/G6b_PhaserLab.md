## Lab verdict addendum — PhaserLab LEARN + CHECK
Good starting instruction (count the dips at 8 stages) that works without sound, but the count is a moving target unless the learner first sets DEPTH to STATIC (advice hidden in PRO TIPS), and "dips" / "notches" are used for the same thing. LEARN carries three entries for controls the rack does not have. CHECK contains two of the best wrong-answer replies in the app (Q1, Q4), plus the usual repeat of EXPLORE's cards and no next step.
