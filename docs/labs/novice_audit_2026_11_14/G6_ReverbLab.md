## Lab verdict — ReverbLab

**Beginner's journey:** A friendly card ("clap in a hall") leads into the most code-worded rack in the set: RT60, PRE, DAMP, a dB view and a 90-word caption. The slope picture is accurate but abstract (a fade drawn as a straight ramp). The learner can still see "longer = gentler slope" by dragging, and the pre-delay vocal question gives a reason to care.

**3 biggest orientation problems**
1. RT60 as the name of the first control and readout.
2. dB VIEW as the only picture (fade drawn as a straight ramp).
3. Very dense hero caption before any action.

**3 best things**
1. Intro card in everyday words.
2. "RT60 is a time, not an amount" — corrects a real misconception.
3. PRE-DELAY vocal problem in CHECK YOURSELF.

**First 30 seconds rewrite**
Card as now. Picture: a hand-clap spike with a fading cloud after it; pointer on DECAY: "Drag me: small room → big hall. Watch the cloud last longer." Then: "PRE-DELAY: a little gap before the room answers — it keeps a voice clear."
