**WHAT'S LEFT page (after FINISH):** said "14 chapters not done yet" after I had opened and worked in all fourteen. A chapter counts only when its own small task is completed (a mini challenge, an UNDERSTANDING CHECK, "open 3 claims", I'VE COMPARED THEM …), and that rule is never stated in one place. A learner who read every chapter would feel cheated. Its intro card also mentions "REVIEW ▾", which I did not see on the page. Suggestion (M): show a ✓ on the stepper / chapter list the moment a chapter's task is done, and end IN THIS CHAPTER with "To finish this chapter: …".

## Lab verdict — TuningLab

**Beginner's journey:** Chapter 1 has the best opening sentence in my group ("An octave is simple: double the frequency. The difficult question is where to place every note between."), and chapters 2, 3, 6 and 7 are hands-on with clear pictures (a snapping fader, a mini challenge, an ADD FIFTH game, the spiral that misses C). From chapter 5 on, BASIC mode still shows algebra (2^(1/3), t = √5/2, ⁴√5, r¹² = 2), and chapter 10 (meantone) is where a beginner would leave. The tuning they actually use (equal temperament) arrives only in chapter 11, and the "why this matters in real audio work" list only in chapter 13. At the end, WHAT'S LEFT says all 14 chapters are not done, though they were all visited.

**3 biggest orientation problems**
1. BASIC mode is not basic enough: formulas and algebra appear in readouts, trays and cards from Ch 5 onward.
2. The reason to care (your piano is equal-tempered; pitch correction, synths and choirs depend on this) comes in Ch 11–13, not Ch 1.
3. "Done" is invisible: no per-chapter "to finish" line and no ✓, so WHAT'S LEFT can contradict the learner's sense of progress; dock keys say "LOCKED" when they mean "not yet".

**3 best things**
1. Ch 1's framing sentence and the rail-over-keyboard picture.
2. Game-like steps with instant feedback (snapping fader, ADD FIFTH, ÷2 floors, the red B♯ spiral).
3. Ch 13 misconceptions ("decide before you open") and Ch 14 "apply" challenges.

**First 30 seconds rewrite:** "Your piano, your guitar and your phone are all tuned the same way — but it isn't the only way, and it isn't 'perfect'. In this lab you'll build four tuning systems note by note and see why each one keeps some notes sweet and lets others go sour. Start: drag UPPER until it says 'fifth'." Plus a chapter list that shows ✓ and labels Ch 10 as "deeper (optional)".
