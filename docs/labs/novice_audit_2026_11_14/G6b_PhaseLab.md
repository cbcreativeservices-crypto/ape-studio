## Lab verdict addendum — PhaseLab LEARN + CHECK
The lab with the fewest controls and the most powerful silent demonstration (flip + mono = flat output and correlation −1). LEARN still starts the beginner at a Greek-letter formula and never draws the "different shift per frequency" idea; later steps need exact fader values that are hard to hit. CHECK is focused on the lab's core idea, with good scale-style feedback on Q4, but repeats EXPLORE's card as Q1 and ends with no next step.
