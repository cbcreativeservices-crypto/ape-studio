# G5b · DJ Systems Lab (DJSystemsLab) · hands-on follow-up to G5

Auditor G5b, 2026-11-14. Scope: hands-on use of screens 2-24 (G5 read every screen's words and intro card and used screen 1 only; its first-look, words and suggestions in `G5_DJSystemsLab.md` are not repeated). Web preview tab-3 `http://localhost:8142/?g5b=5#labpreview/DJSystemsLab`, 390 x 844, member. SOUND OFF throughout: PLAY keys never reached the speakers (the Sound Safety warning was never accepted); every hearing-based step is judged from the meters, the drawing and the words.

Format per screen: what I pressed, what happened (feedback), where a beginner would stick, then numbered suggestions (S/M/L, SHARED where it is the frame). Overwhelm and first look are G5's unless a hands-on detail changes them.

Shared frame finding (seen first on screen 2, applies to every screen): **the dock trays "stay open — compare while you watch" and cover the lower half of the lesson.** With the EQUIPMENT tray open, my taps on the CHECK YOURSELF answers and on START THE FAULT landed on tray items instead (they picked AUDIO INTERFACE in the tray). Only the tray's own ✕ ("Close") closed it; the wide "Close the tray" strip did not. A beginner who opens a tray, then scrolls down to answer the check, will tap the tray by mistake and not know why the answer did not register. Suggestion: close the tray when the learner scrolls the lesson or taps outside it, or dock it above the page's own buttons. M, SHARED.

## DJ SYSTEMS LAB · G5b verdict on hands-on use (screens 2-24)

**What a beginner experiences when they press things.** The trays are the lab's teachers: almost every tray item carries a one-line meaning, LOCKED keys say what to do first, and every fault ends with a "Fixed." paragraph that explains the cause in plain words. Faults are real first-gig mistakes (software sending to the laptop, phono into line, master into the booth monitor, loading onto the on-air deck, nothing cued, channel assigned to the wrong crossfader side, sampler on the on-air deck, effect on the wrong channel, deck 2 on the wrong USB pair). I fixed 13 of them hands-on. What gets in the way is the frame, not the content.

**3 biggest hands-on problems**
1. **Popups that arrive late and take the next tap.** The Sound Safety warning reopens on nearly every screen when playback starts (it re-asked on screens 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 15, 16, 18, 19, 20, 22) and the intro card arrives 1-3 s after the page: on screens 17-21 my first tap was swallowed by one or the other.
2. **Trays that stay open over the lesson.** Taps meant for CHECK YOURSELF answers and START THE FAULT landed on tray items (screen 2); only the tray's small ✕ closes it.
3. **Silent goals.** Many "do X" goals tick only while a deck is playing (screens 3, 7, 14, 15, 17), and PREDICT on screen 23 marks right answers wrong when nothing is on air, with no message saying "press PLAY first". Some lanes have −/+ steps (TRIM, TEMPO, GAIN) and others do not (LOW, EQ): inconsistent.

**3 best things**
1. "Fixed." paragraphs that turn each fault into a rule ("An effect only does something to the signal that passes through the place it sits.").
2. Readouts in words: CH1 L GETS, VOICE IN "CROWD · NOT IN REC", "→ MIX −∞ dB", B vs A in beats, MIX BUS OVER, HOT-PATCH warnings.
3. Consequences that teach: loading onto the on-air deck stops the crowd's music; a feedback loop is "held quiet" while the real first move is named.

**"First 30 seconds" rewrite (hands-on view):** ask about sound once ("Sound on or off for this session? You can do every step with sound off: the readouts show what you would hear"), show the intro card before the page is tappable, and make every tray close when the learner scrolls back to the lesson.
 screens 2-4 took 12-20 tool round trips each in this preview (every tray item needs a fresh look-up). To reach every screen 5-24 hands-on, from screen 5 on I do on each screen: the main EXPLORE action (open the key the step names and use it), one CHECK question, and START THE FAULT with its symptom and first hint; I fix the fault where the fix is one or two moves. Where I stop short, the entry says so.
