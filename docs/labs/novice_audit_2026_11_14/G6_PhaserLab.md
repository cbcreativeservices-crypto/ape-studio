## Lab verdict — PhaserLab

**Beginner's journey:** A wall of a card (eight sentences) greets the learner, but once on the rack the phaser is the most legible of the three modulation labs: two dips, then four, as STAGES goes up — cause and effect anyone can count. The lesson is framed as "different from the flanger", which only helps someone who did the flanger first.

**3 biggest orientation problems**
1. Longest intro card in the set.
2. Lesson defined by comparison to another lab.
3. Small unexplained labels (RES, SWEEP 0.3·50%, "all-pass corner").

**3 best things**
1. Countable notches that respond to STAGES.
2. "a phaser is not a delay" — plain.
3. LEARN's countable WHERE TO START.

**First 30 seconds rewrite**
Card: "**Phaser** — a swirling 'whoosh' made by a few dips that sweep through the sound." SHOW ME → pointer on STAGES: "Tap 8. Count the dips on OUT." Then SWEEP: "Now they move — that's the swirl."
