### Suggestions (hub, ranked)
1. **Add a "START HERE" first tile or tag per family** (no locks — just a marker): Drums → Kick Drum; Idiophones → Hi-Hat; Aerophones → Trumpet; Chordophones → Acoustic Guitar; Voice & Ensemble → Lead Vocal; Foley/Field/Scientific → Foley Footsteps; Sports & Broadcast → Radio, Podcast and Studio Hosts. (S, high impact, SHARED hub)
2. **Shorten every family blurb to one or two lines** and put the long version behind "MORE ▾". Sports & Broadcast first: "Mics for speech on air and for sport — hosts, interviews, commentators, fields and crowds. Silent; every position is a suggested starting point." (S, copy)
3. **Move the footer note to the top**, under the blurb, as one line: "These are starting points, not rules — silent drawings, no sound." A beginner never scrolls past 24 tiles to read it. (S)
4. **Group tiles inside large families under small sub-headings** that already exist in the data's comments: Drums (Kit · Hand drums · Concert · World · Amps), Idiophones (Cymbals · Hand percussion · Metal · Mallet keyboards · Electric pianos), Foley/Field/Scientific (Foley · Field · Measurement), Sports & Broadcast (Studio voice · On-camera · Sport · Crowd). One idea at a time instead of a 24-tile wall. (M)
5. **"0 pages done" on every tile** reads as a wall of zeros on a first visit; show nothing (or "NEW") until a lesson is started, then "n modules done" (word: modules, to match the lesson strip). No totals. (S)
6. **Give the hub guide an intro card** like the lessons have: WHAT YOU'RE LEARNING ("one lesson per instrument, each the same 8-module journey") and WHERE TO START ("Tap START HERE, or the instrument you work with"). (S, SHARED)
7. **Keep jargon off tile subtitles** (X/Y, ORTF, 3:1, T20/T30/EDT, "air kept apart from structure"); the subtitle should say the job in plain words. (S, copy)
8. Cross-family pointers for "where is my instrument?": a guitar amp lives in Drums (SPK) and Strings (Electric Guitar); electric pianos in Idiophones; location speech in both Foley/Field (F09) and Broadcast (B04/B05). A one-line "See also" on those tiles. (S)

**Hobbyist:** Finds their instrument quickly by the tile names (Kick Drum, Acoustic Guitar, Lead Vocal). Will skim blurbs. May wish for a search box across the 128 lessons. (Search is an M idea, noted, not ranked.)

## Verdict · Miking hubs
**Beginner's journey:** From a family tile the beginner lands on a long paragraph and a grid of equal tiles each saying "0 pages done". They can tap any instrument and everything opens, which is good, but nothing says which lesson to start with, and the larger families (24 tiles) and longer blurbs (Sports & Broadcast) bury the first tile. The everyday name ("Drums", "Strings & Pianos") appears only as a small subtitle.

**3 biggest orientation problems:** (1) no start-here per family; (2) long list-style blurbs, worst on Sports & Broadcast; (3) flat 16–24 tile walls with jargon subtitles and "0 pages done" everywhere.

**3 best things:** (1) every lesson open, no locks, tiles never truncated; (2) "No sound plays; how each placement tends to sound is described in words" said up front; (3) the one-line Miking definition popup on first visit.

**First 30 seconds (rewrite):** Header "DRUMS (Membranophones)". One line: "17 silent lessons — pick your instrument. New to miking? Start with Kick Drum." A START HERE tag on Kick Drum, tiles grouped Kit · Hand drums · Concert · World · Amps, and the "starting points, not rules" line directly under the header.
