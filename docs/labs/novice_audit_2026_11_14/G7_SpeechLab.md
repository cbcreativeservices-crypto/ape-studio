## Lab verdict — SpeechLab
**Beginner's journey:** The most relatable lab in my group: it starts with the learner's own body, asks them to touch their throat and say "sss/zzz", and every later module ends in a microphone situation they can picture (pops, hiss, distance, breaths). The pictures carry the lesson even without sound. It gets more technical in Vowels & Formants and The Distance Effect (formants, dB, inverse-square, cardioid), and finishes with two quizzes in a row.

**3 biggest orientation problems**
1. Vowels & Formants teaches "tongue position" with only a spectrum picture — no mouth.
2. dB values (+6, −14, +42) without a plain anchor.
3. Two back-to-back quizzes; an intro popup on all 12 modules; small copy clash (five stages vs four jobs).

**3 best things**
1. Body-first demos ("finger on your throat"; "say a long sss").
2. Cause → picture → consequence → fix pages (pop filter, sibilance, problem simulator), with links to the next relevant lab.
3. Visible progress (✓ in the module list) and explicit completion rules.

**First 30 seconds rewrite:** Already strong. Lead with module 3's line as the lab's hook: "Put a finger on your throat and say 'sss', then 'zzz'. Feel the buzz? That buzz is your voice. This lab follows it from your lungs to the microphone." Then the anatomy picture.
