## Lab verdict — HarmonographLab
**Beginner's journey:** The most inviting first picture in my group: a real-looking drawing machine. The goal is stated simply and the first action (lock the ratio) works and gives a closed figure. Two things get in the way: the machine is big and the drawing small, so the eye studies wood and arms instead of the figure; and the lab opens slightly detuned, so the very first figure "doesn't close", the opposite of the rule being taught. The dock's value codes (90°·M·R) are unreadable.

**3 biggest orientation problems**
1. Drawing inset too small relative to the machine.
2. Opens on the exception (+1% detune), not the rule (locked).
3. Cryptic dock values (SHAPE 90°·M·R, PLAT).

**3 best things**
1. The machine illustration (a reason to care).
2. RATIO/INTERVAL readouts in words.
3. LEARN's "no amplitude key — here's why".

**First 30 seconds rewrite:** "This old machine draws music. Two swinging arms move the pen. When their speeds make a musical interval — like 3 swings to 2 — the drawing closes into a neat shape. Tap TUNE → 2:1 OCTAVE and watch." The drawing large in the centre; machine as a small picture in the corner.
