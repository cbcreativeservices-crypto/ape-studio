## Lab verdict — ChorusLab

**Beginner's journey:** The card's "one singer turning into a small choir" is exactly right, but the picture then shows one slowly breathing sine wave, not a choir. The silent learner can see MIX → VIBRATO change the wave; the rest (comb notches, beating, 50 Hz spacing) is for experts.

**3 biggest orientation problems**
1. Picture does not match the card's metaphor (no visible "many voices").
2. Default SINE source hides the DELAY key (caption admits it); slow default motion.
3. MIX vs WET naming; flanger comparison before the flanger.

**3 best things**
1. Choir metaphor.
2. Colour key in the hero caption.
3. "100% = VIBRATO" as a named tray choice.

**First 30 seconds rewrite**
Card as now. Picture: one grey wave + two faint drifting copies; pointer on RATE: "How fast the copies drift. Drag it." Then DEPTH: "How far they drift." Then MIX → 100 %: "Only the copies, no original — now it wobbles in pitch instead (vibrato)."
