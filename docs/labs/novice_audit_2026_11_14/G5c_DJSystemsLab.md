# G5c · DJ Systems Lab (DJSystemsLab) · the faults G5b could not fix, retried patiently

Auditor G5c, 2026-11-14. Scope: only the DJ faults and goals G5b left open (`G5b_DJSystemsLab.md` screens 6 partly, 9, 10, 11, 13 second fault, 14, 17, 18, 19, 20, 21, 22, 24). G5's and G5b's words and suggestions are not repeated; each entry below adds what a beginner meets when they actually try to finish the screen.

Setup: built-in browser pane, tab-3 only, `http://localhost:8142/?g5c=2#labpreview/DJSystemsLab`, 390 x 844, member. SOUND OFF throughout: the Sound Safety warning was always closed with KEEP SOUND OFF. Screens reached through the MODULE contents (▾).

**How I moved the drag lanes (tool note, not a finding about the app):** in this preview the lane strips do not respond to a plain tool click or drag (G5b). As the coordinator suggested, I moved them with a scripted slow drag: a touch-style pointer-down on the lane at its current value, 25-40 small pointer moves about 40 ms apart, then pointer-up, run from the page's console. It works on every lane I tried; the lane's value and the readouts change exactly as a finger drag would. One quirk worth knowing for the phone check: the lane moves a little MORE than the finger (a drag across 30 % of the strip moved the value about 40 %), so fine targets may overshoot. Where a screen's step buttons existed I used those instead.

**Waiting rule I used:** after every action that starts playback (PLAY, START THE FAULT, PRACTICE AGAIN) I waited 3-4 s for the Sound Safety warning or an intro card to appear, closed it, and only then tapped the next control. With that rule no tap was swallowed. A beginner does not know to wait; they meet the problem G5b described.
