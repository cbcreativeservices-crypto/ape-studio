## Lab verdict — PhaseLab

**Beginner's journey:** The card explains add/cancel well and the hero caption hands the learner a recipe that makes the sound visibly disappear — the strongest "I did it and I see it" moment of the effect labs. After that, the delay half drops the learner into a Lissajous figure and a correlation meter with code-word labels and a Greek-letter formula on LEARN.

**3 biggest orientation problems**
1. Code-word keys (POL, DLY R, FOLD, Ø).
2. Lissajous + correlation with no plain caption.
3. LEARN's popup starts on φ(f) = −2πfτ.

**3 best things**
1. Recipe caption → flat line (visible silence).
2. Card's add/cancel explanation.
3. CHECK YOURSELF that explains the silence just produced.

**First 30 seconds rewrite**
Card: "**Phase** — two copies of a sound can help each other or erase each other." SHOW ME → "Tap FLIP R. Now tap LISTEN: MONO." OUT goes flat with the label "Silence — they cancelled." Then: "Flip back, and slide DELAY R — now only some pitches cancel. That's phase."
