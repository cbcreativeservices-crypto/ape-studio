## Lab verdict — GateLab

**Beginner's journey:** The newcomer meets a jargon title ("Downward expansion"), the safety sheet a second time, and a moving picture of horn shapes in which the default source hides what three of the five controls do. LEARN then explains the gate beautifully in its first paragraph — "the other side of the line" from a compressor — before burying it under six controls the lab does not have.

**3 biggest orientation problems**
1. Card leads with "Downward expansion" instead of "noise gate" and a reason to care.
2. Default CLICK source makes RANGE / HOLD / RLS look dead (the caption admits it).
3. LEARN CONTROLS lists six "not on this screen" controls in the main list.

**3 best things**
1. LEARN's opening paragraph linking gate and compressor.
2. RANGE tray with plain consequences ("SILENCE", "GENTLE").
3. CHECK YOURSELF on chatter with an "honest fix" framing.

**First 30 seconds rewrite**
Card: "**Noise gate** — it mutes the gaps. When the drum stops ringing, the gate closes and the background hiss disappears." SHOW ME → pointer on THRESH: "Sounds louder than this line get through; quieter ones are turned down. Drag it up and watch the tails between hits disappear." Default source: PINK NOISE bursts (so RANGE and RELEASE show).
