# G8 · Miking Labs lessons (`#labpreview/MikingLesson/<id>`)

Walked 2026-11-14 at 390 x 844, member, sound off (the lessons are silent by design: "Nothing here makes a sound: the lab is silent and shows the physics instead", so nothing was lost to sound off). Owner rules kept: miking content = suggested starting points, no in-app citations, modules never locked. No suggestion below asks for citations or locks.

**Depth, said plainly (cut evenly):** the SHARED lesson layout was judged in full on one lesson (M01 Kick Drum: all 8 modules opened, every step of modules 1, 2 and 4 walked with the dock, predictions, checks, trays and both ⓘ keys; modules 3, 5, 6, 7 entered and read; module 8 read from the guide). ## Part A · The SHARED lesson layout (judged once, on M01 Kick Drum)

### A1 · The frame on every step
- Header: the MODULE title is the big title ("MEET IT — WHERE THE SOUND COMES FROM"); the LESSON name is demoted to a grey subtitle ("Kick Drum: Bass drum: inside, outside, one mic or two"). Two identical ⓘ keys sit side by side: the first opens "LEARN HERE — MEASURE WITH THE RIGHT TOOL / ABOUT THESE STARTING POINTS…", the second the screen guide. Nothing tells them apart.
- Strip: ⏮ · ‹ PREV · "MODULE 1 · STEP 1 / 5 ▾" · NEXT ›. Tapping the readout opens the contents (8 modules with ✓/○, WHAT'S LEFT ›, START OVER (PRACTICE)).
- Rack steps: drawing on top with labels, a bezel row of readouts (PART / TAP ONE, FRONT HEAD / PORTED …), a caption line, ▴ HIDE DISPLAY and ⤢ FULL SCREEN, the words in a scrolling well, the dock at the bottom (a slider plus 2–5 keys).
- Every module starts with "Page N goal: …" and the amber STARTING POINTS box ("These are suggested starting points, not rules…"), and ends with TAKEAWAY + "TO EARN CREDIT · …" + NEXT: <next step> ›.
- Intro cards (WHAT YOU'RE LEARNING / WHERE TO START) appear per step, centered over the drawing; GOT IT or tap-outside closes them.

### A3 · Header ⓘ #1 ("LEARN HERE — MEASURE WITH THE RIGHT TOOL")
It opens with the lesson's own ABOUT THESE STARTING POINTS note (good: "a 22 × 18 in kick … head motion drawn larger … Place real mics with the drummer stopped"), then the app-wide calibration text: "Live tools read through your phone's UNCALIBRATED microphone…", "Light → a dedicated light meter", "Loudness, alignment & DSP → lab-grade metering…". In a silent drawing lesson with no live tool, a beginner reads this as "am I supposed to be measuring something?". When both ⓘ were tapped, the calibration sheet opened on top of the screen guide.

### A5 · Suggestions for the SHARED layout (ranked)
1. **One name for the parts of a lesson.** Today: STAGE (journey map, 6), MODULE (strip, 8), "Page N goal" / "This page is credited" / "pages done" (hub), STEP (strip) and STEP (a dock key). Pick "module" and "step" and use them everywhere; rename the dock key STEP (event stepping) to "EVENT ▸" or "NEXT EVENT". Make the journey map list the 8 modules (grouped under the 6 stages if wanted). (M, SHARED)
2. **Put the lesson name back on top.** Title "KICK DRUM", subtitle "Module 1 · Meet it — where the sound comes from". A beginner should always see WHAT they are miking first. (S, SHARED)
3. **Placement Studio legibility:** draw the mic in a high-contrast colour with a halo, draw the blue starting zones at a visible size on a phone, and make ZONE look like a key only when it does something (or name it "GO TO ZONE ▸" always and open the zone list). (M, SHARED)
4. **Say what to do with silence.** Replace "listen for what changes" / "LISTEN FOR ·" in the silent lab with "In a real room you'd hear: …" or "TENDS TO SOUND:" so the beginner is not told to do the impossible. (S, SHARED, copy)
5. **Mark the advanced modules as optional depth, not locked.** A small tag on modules 5–7 in the contents and on their goal line: "ADVANCED — read it, skim the numbers, come back later". This keeps every module open (owner rule) but tells a beginner it is fine not to master Δt today. (S, SHARED)
6. **Readouts in words first.** Under dB / ms / Hz readouts add a plain word ("PICKUP −16 dB = much quieter"; "1ST NOTCH 436 Hz = a dip in the low-mids"). Expand bezel codes: "DYN·SUPER" → "Dynamic · supercardioid". (M, SHARED)
7. **Bring the step's own card up.** On WATCH/TRY/PLACE steps, show the active card (part, setup, START/TENDS TO) directly under the drawing, above the goal and STARTING POINTS box; show STARTING POINTS once per lesson (Start here) instead of on every module. (M, SHARED)
8. **Two ⓘ keys:** label them ("ⓘ HELP" / "ⓘ ABOUT") or merge; in a silent concept lesson, drop the phone-calibration and light-meter lines from the ABOUT sheet and keep only the lesson's own "about these starting points" note. (S, SHARED)
9. **Dock key names:** SETUP (slider/step) and SETUPS (list) → "NEXT SETUP ▸" and "ALL SETUPS ▾"; let the SETUPS tray wrap long names. (S, SHARED)
10. **First-render flash:** the module's last-step TAKEAWAY and "FINISH · SEE WHAT'S LEFT ›" show for a moment when a module opens; a beginner who glimpses "FINISH" on step 1 is confused. (S, SHARED — rendering)
11. **Tapping the drawing:** let a tap on a part's label select it (it is the first thing a beginner tries; on web it did not respond for me — check on a phone). (S, SHARED)
12. **Units:** module 1 cards use inches only ("22 × 18 in"), later steps cm (in). Use cm (in) from the first card. (S, SHARED copy pattern)
13. **Contents → Module 1 breaks NEXT** (found in Part C, reproduced by hand): reopening Module 1 from the contents makes it "1 / 7" with a big green "FINISH · SEE WHAT'S LEFT ›" as the next button. Fix so the contents always lands on the normal step 1. (S/M, SHARED — high impact: it sends a beginner to the end)
14. **Blank display while the drawing loads:** on Placement steps the display stayed an empty black box for 2–9 s in the web preview while readouts were already filled; show a "drawing loading…" line or outline. Check on a phone. (S, SHARED)
15. **Six-key docks truncate at 390 wide** ("POSITI…", "SIDE…", "SETUP SMALL…") on Placement steps; shorter labels or a second row. (S, SHARED)
16. **Words name the wrong key** in places: "STEP through the stages" with a "STAGE ▸" key (M11 Channel plans). Quote the key exactly. (S, copy)
17. **Size the lesson for the learner** (from Part C): ~30 steps / ~7,000 words each; say so on Start here and offer the "foundations" stopping point after module 2. (S, SHARED)

**Hobbyist (shared layout):** The setups-on-a-drawing and the Placement Studio are exactly what a home recordist wants; "I already mic kicks" + quick check respects them. They will still trip on SETUP/SETUPS, STEP as a tray, ZONE, and the double ⓘ, and may skip module 6's maths.

## Part C · Every step of every lesson, walked on screen (second pass, at the coordinator's request)

**How:** in my own tab, each lesson was opened fresh from its hub tile (it opens on Module 1 Step 1), then walked with the strip's NEXT › one step at a time to the WHAT'S LEFT screen, and left with DONE · BACK TO LABS (so the next lesson opens as a new screen, as it would for a learner). At each step I waited until the page settled (up to 3 s), closed any intro card with GOT IT, and recorded: the step id (module.step), the words in the well (between the strip and the bottom NEXT line), and flags. The taps are real UI taps (pointer events on the on-screen buttons). The dock controls were NOT operated on every step of every lesson (they were operated on M01 in Part A), so a flag means "read on screen", not "tried every key". Sound: none in the lab.

**Correction, so nobody chases a false lead:** a first version of this walk reused one lesson screen for the next lesson (tapping the next hub tile while the lesson was still open). That carried a variant choice across lessons and made Toms, Headed Tambourine, the cymbals and shakers show "No starting setup for this choice" on Mic It. Opened fresh (directly and from the hub), those lessons show their setups normally (re-checked by hand on Toms: DRUM ▸ RACK PAIR, "1 of 5 · one mic"). That was my harness method, not the app. All numbers below come from the clean walk.

**Flag key:** P = a PREDICT FIRST card · Qn = n answerable questions on the step · listen = the words tell the learner to listen / LISTEN FOR in a silent lab (the standard "Put the mic up, listen…" box excluded) · labref = a cross-reference by lab number ("Lab 2", "Lab 5") · numN = N dB/Hz/kHz/ms/Δ terms in one step's words (shown when ≥ 3).

### Findings that hold for every lesson walked (not repeated per row)
- **Length.** A lesson is 28–34 steps and about 6,100–7,700 words of reading (M01: 29 steps, 7,136 words). At an adult reading pace that is 30–40 minutes of reading per lesson before any dock play, and there are 128 lessons. The busiest single step is usually module 8 step 2 (Practice · Your setup, 410–490 words) or module 6 step 1 (420–520 words). Suggestion: tell the learner the size on the Start-here step ("about 30 short steps — you can stop after module 2 and come back") and let module 2's end say "You now have the foundations; modules 3–8 go deeper." (S, SHARED)
- **Flash on module entry.** In every lesson, entering module 2 (2.1) and module 3 (3.1) first showed the previous module's TAKEAWAY and a "FINISH · SEE WHAT'S LEFT ›" button before the new step drew (seen in every walk; about half a second in the web preview — check on a phone). (S, SHARED)
- **NEW, reproduced by hand — reopening Module 1 from the contents breaks its NEXT.** On a lesson's Start-here step, open the contents (tap "MODULE 1 · STEP 1 / 6 ▾") and tap "Module 1": the strip changes to "1 / 7" and the big green bottom button becomes **"FINISH · SEE WHAT'S LEFT ›"** instead of "NEXT: LEARN · WHAT IT IS ›" — on every step of module 1, not just a flash (Hi-Hat, opened directly by `#labpreview/MikingLesson/I01a`; screenshot `tool-results/mcp-Claude_Browser-blob-1794652776558-q1fe3l.jpg` in this session). A beginner who uses the contents to "start again" and taps the big button jumps to the end of the lesson. (S/M, SHARED — likely the same cause as the flash.)
- **"listen" in a silent lab** appears on 3.3 (By property, not brand), 4.2 (Place the mic) and 4.3 (How zones work) in nearly every lesson, plus lesson-specific spots in the rows. (S, SHARED copy)
- **Predict-first and checks are everywhere:** PREDICT FIRST on 1.4, 3.2, 4.2, 5.1, 6.1, and 1–6 questions on most CHECK/PRACTICE steps; module 7 (Troubleshoot) holds 6–7 symptom cards. Good spacing of retrieval practice.
- **Module 6 (two mics / two sources)** carries 8–12 dB/ms/Hz/Δ terms per step in most lessons: the maths wall from Part A repeats in every lesson.
- **Intro cards:** after the first lesson, the step intro cards stopped appearing (each step kind's card shows once per device, not once per lesson). A beginner who skipped them in lesson 1 never sees them again unless they tap ⓘ.

## LAB VERDICT · Miking Labs (hubs + 128 lessons)

**Beginner's journey:** A newcomer meets seven families with Greek names, picks an instrument they know (Kick, Acoustic Guitar, Lead Vocal) and lands on an excellent Start-here page: what the lesson is, that it is silent, a six-stage map, and a "New to miking / I already mic" choice. Modules 1 and 2 are plain, picture-led and full of predict-then-check moments, and a beginner will enjoy them. From module 3 on, the same 29-step, ~7,000-word journey turns into engineer readouts (dB, ms, Hz, Δt, nulls), dock keys with near-identical names (SETUP / SETUPS, STEP / step), a Placement Studio that is busy and sometimes hard to see, and "listen for…" lines in a lab that cannot play a sound. Most beginners will stop in module 4 or module 6 — and, because every lesson repeats the same 8 modules, they will not know that modules 1–2 of ten more instruments would suit them better than modules 5–8 of one.

**3 biggest orientation problems**
1. No "start here" at family or lesson-list level, and no sense of size: each lesson is ~30 steps / ~7,000 words with nothing saying "modules 1–2 are the foundation; the rest is depth you can come back to".
2. Naming and controls: STAGE / MODULE / Page / STEP for the same things, SETUP vs SETUPS, a STEP dock key that opens a tray, a ZONE key that is sometimes a dead readout, two unlabelled ⓘ keys; plus the contents → Module 1 jump that turns NEXT into "FINISH · SEE WHAT'S LEFT".
3. Silence and numbers: "listen / LISTEN FOR" in 126 of 128 lessons, and 8–12 dB/ms/Hz/Δ terms on the two-mic step of nearly every lesson, with no plain-word reading of the readouts.

**3 best things**
1. The Start-here step (journey map, silence stated, beginner vs experienced fork) and the plain "What it is / Where you meet it / What it does in the music" cards that respect every tradition.
2. PREDICT FIRST before experiments, checks with reasons and "Choose again", clear credit lines — and every lesson open, no locks.
3. Drawings that teach safety and physics at a glance (keep-clear zones, the kick strike sequence, the singer and the podcast desk), and systems-thinking readouts like Channel plans' CHANNELS / STANDS / PHANTOM / OPEN MICS.

**First 30 seconds (rewrite suggestion)**
On the family hub: "New to miking? Start with Lead Vocal (or Kick Drum / Acoustic Guitar). Each lesson is silent and drawn; modules 1–2 (about 10 minutes) give you the foundation." In a lesson, the title reads the INSTRUMENT ("KICK DRUM"), the Start-here step adds "About 30 short steps. Modules 1–2 are the foundation; 3–8 go deeper — come back any time", and the first drawing appears with a label pointing at the part to tap first.
