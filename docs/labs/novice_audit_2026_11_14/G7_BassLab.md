## Lab verdict — BassLab
**Beginner's journey:** A recognisable instrument, a plain term card, and one slider that makes "shorter string = higher note" visible with a percentage — the clearest cause-and-effect in my group. Harmonics mode adds a second idea without clutter. The rough edges are small: a doubled word in the fret bubble, interval names without plain glosses, a sound-wave wavelength line that distracts from the string, and a LEARN intro that points to the formula first. Without sound, the "hear the octave" payoff is missing, but the readouts carry the logic.

**3 biggest orientation problems**
1. Interval names (MINOR 3RD, TRITONE, PERFECT 5TH) with no plain gloss.
2. LEARN intro points to FORMULA early.
3. Neck orientation (headstock right) can confuse players; "FRET FRET" glitch.

**3 best things**
1. Real bass picture + LENGTH % readout.
2. Fraction strip right above the frets.
3. Term card wording.

**First 30 seconds rewrite:** "Press a string and less of it can swing — so the note goes up. Slide FRET to 12: half the string, double the pitch (one octave)." With the finger dot pulsing at fret 12 as a target.
