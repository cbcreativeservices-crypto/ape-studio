2. A DI's supplier is kept in two places and one packet can print "Band" and "Venue" for the same box; and the packet's "each house mix … 6 outputs" sentence is false for stereo mixes (S1).
3. The salsa pianist's mix asks for a kick the band does not have (S1); and the packet runs 3 to 7 pages because every row repeats defaults, so the two-minute read and the single-page compact option are not there (S2).

5. Sound: the worked example and all three "what if" totals, the no-inference rules in code, stage directions, and the honesty of DRAFT / Ready to advance.
