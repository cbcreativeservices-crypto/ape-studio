Scope: `src/screens/lab/mixingGuides/` (hub, guide screen, search, read record), the 50 generated guides under `data/guides/`, the conversion (`scripts/mixing-guides/`), `docs/labs/mixing/MIXING_GUIDES_LAB.md`. Errors that are in the owner's source are corrected to the physics and listed here so the owner can carry them back to the documents.
