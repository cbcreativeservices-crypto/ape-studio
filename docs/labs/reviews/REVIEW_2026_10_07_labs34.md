# Review — Miking Labs 3 and 4 after the restructure (2026-10-07)

**Scope.** Lab 3, the aerophones (A01–A12, 19 lessons), and Lab 4, the chordophones (C01–C15, 23 lessons).
In the registry these are 38 lessons; the sub-lessons share some ids.
The pages checked were the restructured ones:

- MEET IT;
- STARTING SETUPS;
- MICROPHONES (the mic drawn on the instrument);
- the Placement Studio's START FROM;
- the quick check;
- what's left.

**Branch:** `review-labs34`, based on `final-lab` 456064b1.

**Method.**

- Every STARTING SETUP of every variant was dumped: role, mic type, pattern, mount, pose, distance from its reference surface, zone words and tendency line. That is 89 variant-lists.
- Each setup was checked:
  - against what a working engineer starts from;
  - whether each mic's type is one its zone takes;
  - whether a pair's mics shadow each other along one line.
- Every quick check (6 items × 38) was read for repeats.
- In the web preview at 412 × 915 (`#labpreview/MikingLesson/<id>`, own server on port 8217), these screens were looked at:
  - C11 on the short stick, normal and FULL SCREEN at 1× and 3×;
  - C11 grand, setup 4 (the stereo pair), before and after the fix;
  - C11 upright, TWO MICS;
  - A04a bell up, ONE MIC;
  - C06a, TWO MICS.

**Two experts.**

- **AE** — a senior audio engineer: studio and live, 20+ years.
- **CL** — a cognitive-learning and instructional-design reviewer.

**Owner rules held throughout.**

- These are suggested starting points. There are no names, citations or badges, and the standard line is unchanged.
- Credit is never removed, and no navigation was changed.
- Every setup is still built only from each lesson's own zones. The only drawing defaults are:
  - the bass mic of the upright split pair, which mirrors the treble mic;
  - the lever harp's lower spot, placed the same way as the pedal harp's.
- The Miking and Mixing labs stay hidden on store builds.
- The label ratchet is untouched.

### Checked and correct (AE)

- **Saxophones.** A dynamic 5–10 cm above the bell, aimed at the holes. The pair adds a large condenser 45–60 cm in front. Over the shoulder is the studio view, the clip is the stage view, and the bari "triangle" is correct.
- **Trumpet and trombones.** A dynamic 30–60 cm off axis. The pair uses a farther condenser. The clip goes on the bell, never on the slide.
- **Clarinet, oboe, bassoon.** A third of the way up the bore, then the front omni, the clip aimed at the keys, and the bassoon's 45° view from the right.
- **Guitars, mandolin, ukulele, banjo.** The 12th fret or neck joint at 15–40 cm, the bridge or hole closer, and clips at 3–9 cm.
- **Violin, viola, cello.** In front and above at 0.5–1.2 m. The holder sits behind the bridge, and nothing clamps on the bridge.
- **Harp.** About 60 cm from the board, the pillar spot, and the miniature at the second hole only with the owner's agreement.
- **Electric guitar amp.** The dust-cap edge at 1.5–5 cm. The open-back rear mic has its polarity switched. The far mic is at 60–90 cm.
- **Clavinet amp, and the sitar and veena positions.** Correct.
- **Grand piano.** The treble + bass spaced pair at 20 cm (the bass mic 35 cm back), the curve view and the polarity note under the board are correct.
