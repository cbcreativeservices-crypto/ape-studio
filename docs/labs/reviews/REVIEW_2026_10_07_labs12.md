# Miking Labs 1–2 review — the restructured pages (2026-10-07)

Two reviewers, one pass:

- **(A)** a senior audio engineer, studio and live;
- **(L)** a cognitive-learning and instructional-design reviewer.

**Scope.** Lab 1 (Membranophones: M01–M13, plus SPK, which sits in Lab 1) and Lab 2 (Idiophones: I01a–I12). The review covers the pages the 2026-10-06 restructure built:

- MEET IT;
- STARTING SETUPS;
- MICROPHONES, with the mic drawn on the instrument;
- the Placement Studio's start-from;
- the quick check and the what's-left screen after the restructure (`engine/restructure.ts`, `engine/setups.ts`, `engine/progress/creditMap.ts`).

**Method.**

- Every starting setup in every variant was dumped from `startingSetups()` and read. That means 40 lessons, 85 variants, and each setup's role, mic type, pattern, zone, distance, aim words and line.
- The composed MEET IT / STARTING SETUPS words were dumped, along with each page's checks and each lesson's served six-item quick check.
- The pages were opened in the web preview at 412×915, at `#labpreview/MikingLesson/<id>?page=setups&setup=n`.

**Binding rules.** These were kept throughout:

- starting points only;
- no citations or brand names;
- the standard line;
- credit is never removed;
- navigation is never blocked;
- silent labs;
- the Rack Unit;
- the label ratchet stays 0;
- `MIKING_PUBLIC` stays untouched.

The OWNER REVIEW items already in `CORRECTIONS_LOG.md` were not overridden.

**Counts.** CRITICAL 0 · MAJOR 4 · MINOR 15. Every major finding is fixed, and so are 10 of the minor ones. The remaining minor items are owner decisions.

## What was checked and is right

- Every drawn mic sits at its zone's own start pose, with a type that zone takes, and starts clear of every part (pinned by `mikingRestructure`).
- The aims (amber) and distances (white) agree with each zone's words.
- Snare top and bottom, the open-back cabinet's rear mic (polarity switched), the cajón front and port, and the tabla one-mic-per-drum setups are standard working starts.
- The hearing, phantom-power and clearance safety items are intact and critical in every quick check.
- The standard line appears word for word on MEET IT, STARTING SETUPS and the Placement Studio.
- Credit is never removed: `creditMap` reads instrument and sound as MEET IT, and setting as STARTING SETUPS.
- The setups page carries ≤ 4 new ideas per card (role, mic, start, tends-to).
- Text is ≥ 11 pt, and roles are named in words, never by colour alone.
