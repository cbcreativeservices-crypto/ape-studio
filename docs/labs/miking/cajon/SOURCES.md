## Disagreements
- CAJ-D1 front distance: Shure 6–7 in, dead centre vs White 30–40 cm from just below the top edge.
- CAJ-D2 rear: Shure inside the hole (with a pressure caution) vs White 20 cm out at ~45°. The
  lesson's own rule ("no loose mic inside the box"; clamp only if designed for it — MPMCC) governs
  the app: the "inside the hole" position is available only with the clamp.
