| Doppler (F08, `path.ts`) | v/(v − v_s·cos φ) with c = 343.21 m/s. 1.4 m/s gives +7.08 / −7.04 cents; 20 m/s gives about +104 / −98 cents. "A whole tone needs a far faster source" checks out: about 37 m/s. "An octave needs a source near the speed of sound": c/2 approaching, c receding. Panning never makes Doppler. |
| Parabolic dish (F07, `dish.ts`) | Paraboloid z = r²/(4f), every axial ray through the focus. The capsule sits at the focus, facing the dish. "Little help below c/D": 602 Hz for 57 cm, 587 Hz and 686 Hz for the presets; 60 Hz needs about 5.7 m and 6 kHz about 5.7 cm (the item's whys). The wavefronts in the capture are 17 cm apart at 2 kHz, which is correct. No gain curve is drawn (O-9). |
| Stereo pairs (F05, F06, F08) | X/Y 90°, by level, no comb. ORTF 170 mm / 110° included (55° a side), recording angle about 95°. M/S: L = M + S, R = M − S, and the Side cancels in mono. Spaced omnis comb in mono. Close + room is two distances, not left/right. |
| Lightning, power lines, wildlife | "30 minutes after the last lightning or thunder"; rain shelters, sheds and open vehicles are not safe. 3 m (10 ft) from overhead lines, "farther if unsure, if unsure do not raise it". 25 yd / 100 yd shown as one agency's example, local rules first, "if it reacts you are too close". The same words are used in F06, F07, F09, F10 and F12. |
| Hearing | 85 dBA over 8 h, 3 dB exchange, measured where the person listens. A mic's max SPL is "not a hearing limit" (every lesson). |
| Shotgun (F01–F05, F09) | The base is supercardioid; it narrows only in the highs ("a simplified picture"); it does not remove reflections indoors. F02's close detail uses a small supercardioid because the tube reaches 20 cm ahead. |
| Water and electricity (F04) | The splash is marked with the mic absent; a windscreen is not a water barrier; only an immersion-rated hydrophone goes in water; air, water and structure are three labelled paths; wet effects live only with approved containment. |
| Measurement chain (F11) | Free-field, pressure and random-incidence fields differ in the highs (λ at 10 kHz ≈ 3.4 cm against a 12.7 mm capsule). "Omni" names a pattern, not a field. Prepolarized capsules use CCP or the approved unit; never polarize them. dBFS is not SPL. The after check is read unadjusted. One 1 kHz check checks the sensitivity only. The calibrator never goes to an ear (94 dB ≈ 1 Pa). |
| Levels (F12) | 10·log10(10^6.2 − 10^5.6) = 60.74 dB. The LAeq of 50 and 70 dB for half the time each = 67.0 dB. Subtraction is refused inside the method's limit (lab default 3 dB, "your method sets this"). A fast maximum is not a peak. L90 cannot remove a source that never stops. A facade position is named, never "free field". |
| Decay (F13) | T20 from −5 to −25 dB ×3 needs 35 dB of range; T30 from −5 to −35 dB ×2 needs 45 dB; EDT from 0 to −10 dB ×6. Each fit's end sits 10 dB above the floor. With 40 dB, T20 is valid and T30 is refused. A measurement through the PA is "system + room". |
| Systems (F14) | Arrival = path ÷ c, so the nearest source arrives first. A delay finder takes the strongest arrival. A post-processor tap leaves the processor out. A window of T resolves about 1 ÷ T (5 ms ≈ 200 Hz). An alignment at one seat changes at the next. Seat heights 1.2 / 1.7 m. |
| Product sound (F15) | A guarded fan only. The exclusion zone and the airflow are keep-outs. A contact sensor reads vibration in its own units. A shared peak is a clue, not a path. A phone with auto-gain gives a perspective, not a comparison. |
| Arrays (F16) | f_max = c ÷ 2d: 60 mm gives 2.86 kHz (half a wavelength at 29 kHz ≈ 5.9 mm; at 290 Hz ≈ 59 cm, as the whys say). The mirror gives the same Δt, and a third element off the line breaks it. The probe reads cos θ. Nyquist (48 kHz keeps content below 24 kHz). 1 µPa against 20 µPa is 26 dB, and equal intensity differs by about 61.5 dB in all; nothing teaches subtracting. |
| Ambisonics (F10, `spatial.ts`) | The A-format order FLU, FRD, BLD, BRU (the manual decides); FuMa is W, X, Y, Z with W 3 dB down; ambiX is W, Y, Z, X. Gains are matched and linked; ".1" is never a full-range field channel. The close mic is about 17 ms earlier at about 6 m. The Double M/S positive lobe is marked. |
