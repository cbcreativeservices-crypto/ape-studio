## a. Facts read today

| Fact | Value (exact) | Source | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Not too close (Don White) | "Mic'ing things too closely doesn't give sounds the room ambiance"; they place mics farther "for the sound to have some detail, to catch the natural reverb of the room or space, so they wouldn't have to add lots of reverb later" | OUP-MW | Medium |
| Location Foley (Andy Malcolm) | "Location Foley is amazing because of the realism" (returned to the filming locations, *Agnes of God*, 1985) | OUP-MW | Medium |
| Close + room blended live (Ben Cross) | "We have both close and room mics set up which are mixed live to achieve the effect we're after"; "the right perspective, depth and level so that it sits in the 'space' of the scene" | ASE-CROSS | Medium |
| Mix 1997 stations | two stations described in the text read today ("On one, we had hemostats and scalpels … on the other station we had nonmetallic things like gloves and gowns") | MIX-1997 | Medium |
| Mix 1997 wider pickup | the engineer (Ruschak) wanted "more air between the Foley and the microphone": "even with a medium or short capsule on a shotgun mic, you still get this really tight pattern" (chose a non-shotgun condenser) | MIX-1997 | Medium |
| Live opera Foley | "A special booth is constructed stage left … so that the foley artist is visible to the audience"; "Each sound is cued live from the actions of a performer, the timing of the spoken dialogue or a cue in the music"; props incl. "a watering can, 10 bottles … a slab of concrete and a thunder sheet" | ENO-FOLEY | Medium |
| Monitors in the rejection | "Aligning floor monitors and side fills with the directional microphone's angle of rejection will give the maximum gain before feedback" | S-3REASONS | High |
