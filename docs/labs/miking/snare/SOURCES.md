## Disagreements log (both sides kept)

- **D-S1, Audix i5 reference surface.** DP Elite 8 sheet (archived): "2 inches above the rim". Current i5
  sheet: "about 2 inches above the head". 2 in = 50.8 mm either way.
- **D-S2, Shure snare distance.** SM57 guide: "2.5 to 7.5 cm (1 to 3 in.) above rim of top head". Shure
  articles 2022 and 2026: "a good 4 inches away from the snare". Different documents, different aims
  (the articles say it captures "the whole drum sound").
- **D-S3, which approach is "most common".** Shure 2022: two mics. Shure 2026: top only. Both quoted.
- **D-S4, i5 weight.** 248 g (i5 sheet) vs 250 g (DP Elite 8 sheet).
- **D-S5, bottom-mic polarity.** Sennheiser: "must be phase-reversed". Shure 2026: "invariably out of
  phase … will usually produce a better result". Shure booklets: "may sometimes achieve a 'fatter'
  snare drum sound. This effect will change dependent on mic locations." The lesson's "teach the check,
  not a law" is consistent with all three.
