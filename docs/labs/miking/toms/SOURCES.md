## Disagreements log

- **D-T1, starting heights for tom close mics.** Beta 56A/SM57 25–75 mm "above drum heads";
  e 904 30–50 mm; DM20 38.1–76.2 mm; Audix 1–2 in (25.4–50.8 mm) to "the bottom of the grill".
  Different mics, different reference points (Audix measures to the grille bottom).
- **D-T2, rack tom bolt counts.** Yamaha's tom table repeats the bass/floor table; not used.
- **D-T3, Beta 56A weight.** Guide: "0.468 kg (1.6 lbs)"; product data 468 g = 1.032 lb.
