## Disagreements
- GLK-D1 bar material: Yamaha general article "aluminum alloy"; YG-2500/YG-1210/Adams/Jenks "steel".
- GLK-D2 YG-2500 range wording: spec "4 1/3 octaves" and "3 1/3 octaves" on the same page; manual
  "3 1/2 octaves". C5–E8 is 3 octaves + a major third (DERIVED) → "3 1/3" is right; "4 1/3" is a typo.
- GLK-D3 YG-2500 height: spec 33 1/2–41 3/8 in; manual 85–105 cm (= its own "33-1/2"-44-3/8"").
- GLK-D4 YG-2500 bar size / weight: spec 1 1/4 × 3/8 in (31.75 × 9.525); manual 32.5 / 9 mm;
  70 lbs vs 36 kg (79 lbs).
