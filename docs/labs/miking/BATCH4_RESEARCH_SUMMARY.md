## 2. Corrections to the lesson texts

All twelve old-template lessons: strip "Pro Audio Training Academy" (house rule, as Batch 1).

**Survey items resolved**
- **Clavinet L41 monitor null is backwards**: aim the rejection null **toward** the loudest monitor
  (Sennheiser e 906: put monitors "in the angle area of the highest cancellation"). CONFIRMED wrong.
- **Clavinet L6 mechanism**: not "a hammer presses a string against a pickup-facing surface". Hohner:
  "a plunger underneath touches the string and presses it on to an anvil"; release frees the
  "wool-wound part" which mutes the string; "Magnetic pick-ups are situated at the other end of the
  string". Range "contra F to e'''", 60 keys, one string per note.
- **Oud and veena copied ranges**: both use 35–60 / 20–35 / 70–120 cm; label them as shared trials or
  differentiate. "70–120 cm (2–4 ft)" is a loose conversion (2.3–3.9 ft). The only oud-specific numbers
  reachable today: Mike's Oud Forums "a foot to a foot and half" (30–46 cm), "maybe 8 inches" (20 cm),
  live "about 5 inches or less from the main rose". The lesson's Fletcher "1–2 ft" and "at least 18 in"
  come from ProSoundWeb (403) — unverifiable here.
- **Missing hearing notes**: add the OSHA/NIOSH line to C01 (none today), C09b, C09c, C10, C11, C12,
  C13, C14, C15. C11 should add DPA's "more than 130 dB close to the hammers".

**Wrong or misattributed facts**
- **C05a Banjo**: "Shure's live-sound guide describes about 1 ft (30 cm) from the bridge" — not in the
  booklet. Banjo rows are "3 inches from center of head", "3 inches from edge of head", "Miniature
  microphone clipped to tailpiece aiming at bridge". The ribbon in the bluegrass article **is named**
  (KSM313). DPA's "two omnis at 30-40 cm … distance" is ambiguous (spacing or distance) — say so (also C05b).
- **C02**: e 906 "designed to hang in front of an amp" is not in either e 906 manual; ref [2] PDF is
  dead (404) → cite the archived copy or the online manual v1.3 04/2026. e 906's start is "the middle
  between dome and edge" (± 30° toward the edge), adjacent to but not the same as Mills' dust-cap line.
- **C01**: Shure gives the sound hole the **same** 6–12 in as the 12th fret ("Place near the sound
  hole for a full sound") — "closer to the sound hole" → "near". DPA's 4099 guide says "where the
  fretboard meets the body, typically above the 12th fret" — true for 12-fret (nylon) bodies only.
- **C11 Piano**: add DPA's 45° ("pointed at 45° downwards, towards the pianist"); state ORTF as 17 cm /
  110° included (DPA's own page writes "±110°", a slip). Ref [3] is correct for the one-mic sound hole
  ("A single SM58 pointing into one of the soundboard holes"); the Shure booklets add numbered rows
  (12 in above middle strings, 8 in from hammers; 8 in above treble; 6 in, short stick; boundary pair 2/3
  from middle A). Note Shure's soundboard-clamp suggestion vs the lesson's no-attachment rule.
- **C06b**: Neumann TLM 193 "possible orchestral spot for low strings" is not on Neumann's page
  ("mainly designed for home, project, and broadcast studios") — soften or source. MK 4V confirmed.
- **C06a**: Shure's old booklet also suggests a foam-wrapped mic behind the bridge; the lesson's
  no-foam rule is safer — keep it, note the difference.
- **C08**: the "dust-cap/cone boundary" start is from Mills (guitar), not Shure's bass article; add 5-string
  B0 30.87 Hz; Ampeg's tube-load FAQ wording not found in the fetched FAQ (re-check in a browser).
- **C09a**: cite Shure PGA27's "Strings … 1-6 feet (30 cm - 2 m)" as the published envelope around the
  0.5–1.2 m trial; optional GE-OS "approximately 30cm in front of where the bow rubs the strings".
- **C09b**: DPA names the 4011C in the body text it cites — the lesson body may name it (fixes the
  body/audit mismatch). "1.5–4 ft" for 0.5–1.2 m → "about 1.6–3.9 ft".
- **C09c**: Shure says "one foot from the bridge" (no direction); "in front of" is the lesson's reading.
- **C04**: Peavey manual (ref [4]) is dead; keep model-specific wording out until re-sourced.
- **C10**: DPA suggests partly stuffing harp sound holes with foam; the lesson forbids foam — keep the
  lesson rule with "only with the owner's consent". Add "second sound hole from the bottom" and "top mic
  twice as loud" (≈ +6 dB).
- **C14**: the GE-OS live URL no longer answers; cite the Internet Archive copy (the article does cover
  the sitar). **C15**: "Saravatī Vīnā" is the Met's own title (not a typo); Met 500718 is titled
  "Ranjanî vînâ" — don't call it a rudra veena without a source.
- **C07**: add "(about 30 cm)" and "treble side (cutaway)" to the Taylor engineer's start.

## 3. Shared families to build once

1. **Guitar body family** (`acoustic_guitar/GEOMETRY_PROPOSAL.md` §7, frame G: origin at the saddle,
   +x to the nut, +y toward the treble string, +z out of the top). Parametric outline, depth, scale,
   frets-to-body (fret formula d_n = L·(1 − 2^(−n/12))), openings {round, oval, f-holes, coverplate,
   membrane head, rosettes}, bridge types, cutaway. Variants: steel 6, 12-string, nylon, acoustic bass
   (C07), resonator single/tricone (C03), mandolin A/F, ukulele ×4, banjo pot (C05), oud bowl (C13).
2. **Bowed-string family** (`violin/GEOMETRY_PROPOSAL.md`, frame B: origin at the bridge foot line on
   the top): one parameter row each for violin, viola, cello, double bass; shared anchors (bridge,
   f-holes, tailpiece, contact point), **bow envelope** (contact ± slab, hair travel, frog sphere,
   string-crossing angle), left-hand envelope, three postures (under-chin standing, seated cello,
   standing bass). Clip/holder library: VC4099 (35–55), GC4099 (35–122), C-CLIP, BC4099 (E+G strings),
   MHS6005, Neumann MC 1/2/3/4 (MC 2 strings 0.6–2.3 mm).
3. **Piano family** (`acoustic_piano/GEOMETRY_PROPOSAL.md`): shared 88-key keyboard (52 white × 23.43,
   middle at E4|F4, A4 at +58.6), grand plan/side in frame P (Model D/B/S), upright in frame U (K-52),
   lid with swept keep-out, action/damper keep-out, frame holes, boundary-on-lid, magnets.
4. **Amp = the speaker family** (`speaker_leslie/GEOMETRY_PROPOSAL.md` Part A, frame C) used unchanged
   by C02, C04, C08, C12 (+ Lab 1 Rhodes/Wurlitzer/Harmonica). New cabinet: the '65 Deluxe Reverb
   combo; new zones: e 906 A/B/C, Mills two-mic, live "against the grille", bass 4–18 in band; new
   readouts: r/d separation, unsafe-patch stop (speaker output → mic/line/DI), DI Pre/Post labels.
5. Smaller shared pieces: stereo pair builder (X/Y 90°, ORTF 170/110, A/B), player-envelope kit (seated
   / standing / floor-seated torso, head, hand boxes, feet zones — all ILLUSTRATIVE), mono/polarity panel
   (polarity drawn apart from delay), clip-fit checker.
