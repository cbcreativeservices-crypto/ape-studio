# I01c Crash Cymbal: GEOMETRY PROPOSAL

Frame K, classes: `hihat/GEOMETRY_PROPOSAL.md`. Reuse `CRASH_16` / `CRASH_18` and their boom
stands from `cymbalSpec.ts` (kit plan: crash1 (−80, h 1150, −560) tilt 15°; crash2 (250, h 1200, 360)
tilt 15° — ILLUSTRATIVE). Default lesson cymbal: **crash1 16 in** (the one over the high tom and
near the hat/snare; the lesson's neighbours "snare, high tom, another crash, ride, vocal mic").

| Zone | Region | Class | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `zone.crash.overhead` | the M09 pair | SOURCED option | lesson; M09 |
| `zone.crash.top` | above the bow, aimed down at the bow/edge, 200–350 above the edge plane, outside swing + glancing-stroke envelope | DRAWING DEFAULT (lesson: "a conditional teaching starting point, not a source-established distance") | lesson |
| `zone.crash.under` | below the plate, aimed up at the underside, 100–200 below the edge plane at r 0.5 R on the audience side; mount on the crash stand or own stand | option SOURCED (DPA-KIT, DPA-KILLERS); position DRAWING DEFAULT | |

| Keep-out | Region | Class |
|---|---|---|
| `ko.swing` | ±60 at the edge (family); for `zone.crash.under` ADD a downward flex/rock band of 80 below the edge plane under the whole plate (lesson L15 "the plate can flex downward or rock toward it") | DRAWING DEFAULT (owner) |
| `ko.stroke` | glancing J path: from the strike point at the edge (shoulder, r 0.95 R, drummer side) continuing 250 past the edge and 150 down/away | ILLUSTRATIVE (ZIL-L11 words) |
| `ko.choke` | grip box at the edge (120 × 100 × 80) | ILLUSTRATIVE |
| neighbours | high tom, snare, hi-hat, crash2, vocal mic (drawing default boom at the drummer's head, kit plan `drummer.headTopH` 1300) | ILLUSTRATIVE |

Readouts: distance and aim to bow/edge; time offsets to overheads, tom mic and vocal mic (the
lesson's "reaches overheads, spot, tom and vocal mics at different times") via
`SOURCES_SHARED.md` Δt. Owner: all bands here are drawing defaults; no source gives a number.
